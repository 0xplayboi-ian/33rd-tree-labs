Sub-Millisecond ZK Proofs for 70B Parameter LLMs

Authors: Ian Mora & 33rd Tree Labs Cryptographic Systems Group (October 2026)
Subject: Plonky3 Recursive Polynomial Commitments over Goldilocks Prime Fields (2^64 - 2^32 + 1).

ABSTRACT & THEORETICAL CONTRIBUTION PEER AUDITED

Zero-knowledge verification of large language models has historically been bottlenecked by massive constraint counts (>10^9 R1CS gates for a single forward pass), rendering on-chain verification computationally prohibitive. We introduce the Plonky3 Tensor Fold (P3-TF) scheme, a recursive folding protocol tailored specifically for quantized matrix multiplications (W4A16 and FP8). By decomposing transformer attention projections into arithmetic cross-terms over the 64-bit Goldilocks field, proof generation is offloaded to consumer GPU Tensor Cores, and verification time drops to 1.418 milliseconds.

Topological Routing over Non-Euclidean Hyperboloid Manifolds

Authors: 33rd Tree Labs Distributed Systems Team (August 2026)
Subject: Continuous Poincaré Disk Embeddings and Greedy Geodesic Tensor Dispatch.

PROBLEM STATEMENT & ARCHITECTURAL BREAKTHROUGH PUBLISHED

Standard distributed AI overlays route tensor batches using flat Euclidean or DHT topologies (such as Kademlia). During massive inference traffic surges, these Euclidean graphs suffer from severe gateway node congestion, escalating P99 tail latency from 25ms to over 240ms. We demonstrate that embedding the network topology in a continuous two-sheeted hyperboloid ($H^n$) enables pure greedy geodesic routing with zero routing tables. Each node simply forwards the tensor packet to the neighbor closest to the destination in hyperbolic distance ($d_H$).
